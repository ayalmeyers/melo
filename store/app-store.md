## Description (4,000 max)
Melo is where people who care about music keep score and talk about it.

LOG WHAT YOU LISTEN TO
Rate any album or podcast episode from half a star to five, add a heart for the ones you love, and keep a diary of everything you've heard. Pick your Top 4, the records that explain you.

BRING YOUR HISTORY
Import your Spotify or Apple Music listening history and start with a full diary instead of an empty one.

REVIEWS, HOT TAKES AND THREADS
Write a review, drop a one-line take that people vote on, or start a thread. Every album, artist and genre has its own board, so the conversation lives where the music does.

WIKIS FOR EVERY RECORD
Every album, artist and genre has a wiki anyone can improve, with full history.

MOMENTS
Mark the second the beat switches or the line that got you. Moments are timestamped reactions on tracks and episodes.

CHARTS FROM REAL LISTENERS
See what Melo logged most this week, for everyone or just the people you follow, with movement against last week.

FORECASTS
Call what happens next: chart number ones, surprise albums, breakout artists. The crowd's number weights people by how often they've been right. That's your Ear score.

FIND YOUR PEOPLE
Taste match shows how close your ears are to anyone's. "People who hear like you" finds the rest. Chat one-on-one or in groups and send albums straight into the conversation.

LISTS
Make ranked lists with notes: your albums of the year, every record by one artist ranked, a shelf for a 6am flight.

SHARE ANYWHERE
Post a review straight to your Instagram story with a card made for it. Every link unfurls with a preview.

Works alongside Spotify and Apple Music: open any album in either with one tap.

Melo is moderated. Report or block anyone from any post, comment, profile or message.
